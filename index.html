Where It All Began

Our Story

Diana Burks and Ginger Stockton founded Divine Design Realty to give clients both sides of a great home sale: an agent who knows the contract and the market, and a designer who knows the room.

For 26 years they have worked as real estate agents in Texas, helping families buy, sell, and settle into homes they love. Along the way they learned that the details people remember, a warm entry, a well-lit kitchen, a bedroom that feels calm, are the ones that turn a listing into a home.

Whether you're buying your first place, selling a family home, or finally finishing the living room, they treat it as personal, because it is.

Diana Burks

Co-founder & Realtor

Diana is a Realtor, a wife, and a mom of three who loves helping people move into their next chapter with confidence. She and her husband, Kevin, have been married for 30 years, and she knows firsthand what it takes to make a house feel like home.

Diana pairs a genuine love for people with a strong eye for design. She takes the time to understand how each client lives, finds the home that fits, and helps them see what every room could become. Whether you're buying, selling, or working toward your dream home, she brings the same high standard, creativity, and care to every step.

She believes life is designed to be lived with joy, and she loves helping her clients do exactly that.

Diana

Ginger Stockton

Co-founder & Realtor

Ginger brings more than 13 years of real estate experience and a passion for interior design to her work. After nine years in interior design with a home builder, plus years of staging and flipping homes, she can walk into a house and quickly see what it could be.

That eye helps sellers present their home's best features and helps buyers see the possibilities in their next one. Ginger believes every home has a story and every client deserves thoughtful attention.

Married to her best friend and a mom of two, she knows how much it means to find a home where a family can grow, gather, and make lasting memories.

Ginger
